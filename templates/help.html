Help & FAQs

Find quick solutions to common IT problems.

Internet Not Working

Check your Wi-Fi connection, restart your router, and reconnect to the network.

Computer Not Starting

Check the power cable, charger and power connection. Try restarting your computer.

Software Installation Problem

Check whether you have permission to install the software. Contact your IT administrator if needed.

Printer Not Working

Check the printer connection, paper supply and printer status.

Login Problem

Verify your email and password. If you still cannot log in, contact your IT administrator.

Submit a support ticket and let the IT team help you.
